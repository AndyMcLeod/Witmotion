Settings

Sensor settings are unlocked, written, and saved to flash in one step, the same sequence the WitMotion PC software uses.

Calibration

Accelerometer

Place the sensor on a level, still surface. Takes about 5 seconds.

Heading

Make the current yaw read 0°.

Angle reference

Store the current roll and pitch as the sensor's zero (saved on the sensor).

Configuration

Save the current configuration, or restore factory defaults. A factory reset may change the baud rate and address.

Magnetometer calibration

Keep away from metal and magnets. Press Start, then slowly rotate the sensor a full turn around each of its X, Y and Z axes. Press Finish when the three ellipses look complete.
